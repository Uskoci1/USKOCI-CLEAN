## Evidence gate

Before a major surface is designed, inspect at least **5 relevant examples from 3 independent sources**. Count unique records, not duplicate screenshots of the same observation. Apple topic pages are one publisher; Banani captures remain one source even across products. Homepages, kit advertisements, failed access and LOW-confidence excerpts do not satisfy the gate. Guidelines and documented flows can contribute, but cannot substitute for inspecting relevant rendered product behavior. Numeric eligibility is not design approval or proof that every interaction has been studied.

## Maintenance

Add a dated bounded observation with provenance and all fields; update EVIDENCE.json, group links and counts together. Do not treat access status as researched evidence. Preserve source attribution and never copy proprietary artwork or whole layouts. References expire when the relevant product behavior or platform contract changes.
