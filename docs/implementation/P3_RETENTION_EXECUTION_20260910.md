# P3 execution and privacy — working source, database proof pending

The existing retention registry now has a separate execution capability for one dataset: abandoned AI intake conversations that have never been bound to a Zadatak and have no saved facts, proposals, draft receipts or safety-review evidence. A complete published schedule and this narrow capability are separate receipts. No other AI records, account closure, media cleanup or whole-account retention execution are admitted by this adapter.

The implementation reuses `private.retention_policy_sets`, the existing AI tables, the existing audit owner and `private.marketplace_tick`. It adds a nullable typed execution binding, private holds and fenced jobs. Legal text is not parsed into durations. A binding must explicitly name the supported dataset/action/observed trigger and duration, match its content digest and the active Privacy document, and retain full required-class coverage. Nothing activates or seeds a production policy.

Only newly observed, server-tracked origin and abandonment facts can qualify a conversation. Older records receive no invented abandonment date. Links or evidence permanently retire eligibility. The final worker must recheck policy, source, lease and holds under the same account and parent-row locks before deletion. Delete and completion belong to one transaction; a failed operation restores deleted rows before recording a bounded retry. The implementation and disposable proof are still being reviewed at this checkpoint.

It exposes no engine identifiers, private target identifiers or fabricated deletion result. Account closure is explicitly unavailable because its authority is unfinished.
