# R13 — discovery composition

The person needs to find a suitable nearby task, read its real terms, and open it without losing the search.

## Alternatives examined before implementation

1. Dense utility rows: efficient for known work, but a narrow title competes with price and leaves the human behind the task visually incidental.
2. Image-led editorial cards: attractive when photos exist, but this projection does not carry task photographs. Invented or category stock imagery would misrepresent work and displace useful facts.
3. Open job summaries and one focused map preview: a full-width title, a clear price/capacity decision line, illustrated place/time, then the publisher. This is the selected direction. Real state, uncertainty and ownership remain explicit.

The search opens a white workspace with a stable heading and ruled sections. An expanded section receives room; collapsed sections preserve the selected value. This replaces five floating nested cards over a translucent map, because reading filter choices should not compete with the underlying map. Draft/apply/discard, truthful result counts, accessibility, keyboard handling and every filter's meaning remain intact.

The date mode uses full-width options at large text instead of squeezing “Fleksibilno” into half of a narrow screen. Motion continues through existing press feedback, map camera, native transitions and the draggable sheet; no idle decoration is added.

Map framing is being investigated separately: the initial camera currently freezes overlay estimates before the map has measured its real frame. No geographic facts, location permission rules or area-filter ownership may change with its correction. Evidence and native observations will be added after verification.

## Boundaries

No backend, provider, payment, dependency or real business mutation. No invented photos, ratings, prices, counts or progress. Existing white surface and Inter system retained. Provider attribution remains visible and operable.
