## What P6 does NOT claim
Not a production-scale load proof (the 30k SQL screening of Round67 is SQL-only; the native journeys run 100 rows), not an iOS proof, not a phone proof (the owner asked for the
emulator only), not provider/voice/payment proof. Personal lists (A09, A11, B10) are unpaged reads of one account's own rows and are listed as follow-up packages.
