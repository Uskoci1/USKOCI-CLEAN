**F1, the one that matters.** The live body of `private.availability_is_future` (read on DEV 2026-10-01) is `coalesce(isfinite(s) and isfinite(e) and s<e and e>at_now and (s>at_now or kind in ('TOMORROW_FLEXIBLE','WEEK_FLEXIBLE')), false)`: with no window it is false. In `private.worker_dispatch_time_admitted` (repository text equals the DEV md5) a TOMORROW_FLEXIBLE or WEEK_FLEXIBLE task whose window is not "future" returns `false` before the worker's availability is even read. So **a flexible-day or flexible-week task that is stored without a window can never be dispatched to anyone**, whatever the workers declare. Live DEV (counts only): the 3 TOMORROW_FLEXIBLE tasks ever created all have no window, all EXPIRED, and **41 of 41 dispatch rounds ended `NO_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATES`**; there is no WEEK_FLEXIBLE task. Caveats: n = 3; the harness proposals carried no window (the real AI turn could in principle add one, but the 3 live tasks show it did not); the larger share of the 607 empty rounds on DEV comes from other causes (one of five active workers has a location preference, two have no availability - S01) and is NOT explained by F1.
