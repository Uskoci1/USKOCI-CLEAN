Slice EX05-S01 (docs/implementation/product-v1-closure-20260926/finalization-20260927/EX05_CANONICAL_SCOPE_20261001.md, gap G10): the SQL proofs of text, photo, group and voice chat were last run before B24 (SQLSTATE 40001 became PT409 on DEV, ledger 213 and 214) and before Voice B1 (ledger 215). This table answers, per existing proof and without running anything: what predecessor state does it assume, do those assumptions still hold on the state DEV has now, and what does the slice do about it.

The honest finding is structural. Nearly every existing proof applies the candidate it proves at a recorded history position and asserts the state before it, so NONE of them can run unchanged on a chain that already carries B24 and Voice B1. They stay valid evidence at their own position (the workflow re-runs the ones it can on the exact head) and say nothing about the post-state. The post-state behaviour is therefore re-asserted by UPDATED COPIES in supabase/proofs/ex05_s01/ (the frozen originals are not edited), each with its diff stated, on a disposable chain built from the predecessors the D12 workflow recorded plus the P4 transport candidate and B24 Part 1.

A disposable chain is NOT DEV, a source or CI result is NOT device proof, and a reproduced contract gap is a finding for the owner, never an applied change.

## Legend

- RERUN AT ITS POSITION: the frozen proof, unchanged, at the predecessor position it was written for, on the exact head (jobs `chain` and `d03-position`).
- UPDATED COPY: a new proof under supabase/proofs/ex05_s01/ that asserts the same behaviour on the final chain; the check names are listed per row and every check runs even after an earlier one failed (a dependent check is SKIPPED, never green).
- NOT RE-RUN: the reason is stated in the row; nothing is silently dropped.
- Result column: NOT RUN until the workflow ran; then PASS or FAIL with passed/total checks (RECORDED observations count as passed).
- Verdict codes: FAILS_ON_A_CHAIN_THAT_REPLAYS_B24 (asserts a retired code), HOLDS_AT_ITS_POSITION_ONLY (valid evidence for its own state, cannot run later), HOLDS_FOR_THE_CANDIDATE_PATH_ONLY, UNAFFECTED_BY_B24_AND_VOICE, STALE_AFTER_LATER_PACKAGES.
