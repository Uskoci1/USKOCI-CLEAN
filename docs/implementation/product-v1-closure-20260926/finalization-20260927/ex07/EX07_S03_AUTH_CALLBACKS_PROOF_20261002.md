## Design findings worth the owner's / the next agent's attention (SOURCE reading, not CI)
1. Expired, used or wrong **signup** links open the login form with **no message** (the app ignores the fragment by design). N02 asks for "clear recovery"; the only recovery is signing up again or the resend on the confirmation stage, which is reachable only right after signup. A resend entry on the login form is a labelled proposal in the scope doc, not canonical. `E07` records the behaviour; it does not call it a defect.
2. A warm confirmation callback on the open 'Proveri email' stage does not move the stage (same route parameter; an existing Jest test pins that). `E05` records the stage it observes.
3. The recovery client never signs out the recovery session at the provider after a successful password change (`createRecoveryTransport.dispose()` only aborts). Whether the callback tokens stay usable for **their own** account afterwards is the provider's session rule (`P14`, `E12` observe it; N03 asks to "check the existing session invalidation rule"). Never for another account: `E10`/`E11` prove that the app refuses a recovery callback while another account is signed in (rule `SIGNED_IN`), with a positive control that the same callback is valid at the provider.
