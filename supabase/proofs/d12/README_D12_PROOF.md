With the raw md5 the first run was red on every chain. The DEV scan on 2026-10-02 found no other pinned body with a 64-hex literal, a UUID literal or the project reference (46 of 47 hold no hex literal of 12 or more digits).

## Chain fidelity (the label on every pass line)
The chain is NOT DEV: it lacks pkg051a, PKG-045b P0, P6 rollout v3 and B24 Part 1; OIDs, ledger and digest are chain-internal. "D12 PINS == DEV" claims only the pinned bodies and metadata. A difference in ANY pinned function is red unless explained. Known residuals are pinned, not blessed (`report.residuals`).

**Pins most at risk of a first-run difference** (not verified; each is a function whose DEV body may come from a DEV-only ledger item that the chain's replay does not apply): the four closure functions that the B24 Part 1 candidate (`b24_nonretried_conflicts_part1.sql`, applied to DEV, absent from the chain) names: `private.closure_erasure_assert_current_v5`, `public.rpc_start_account_closure_execution`, `public.rpc_redact_account_closure_step_service` and `private.support_reference_v5` (the chain carries B24 Part 2 only, in relaxed pre-image mode); the world helpers `private.accounts_same_world` and `private.account_visibility_world` (named by `p6_discovery_rollout_v3.sql`, absent from the chain; the round-2 review found only reads of them there, no redefinition); and the metadata (owner, ACL, configuration) of any function that PKG-045b P0 or pkg051a touched. The readiness body also embeds md5 literals of other certified function bodies (20 of them on DEV): a chain whose certified functions differ from DEV's would differ there as well, and its masked fingerprint would then differ (red, by design). Read the first run's pin-gate table (`d12-report.md`, `report.pinGate.allDifferences`) and, to see every difference of a new chain in one cheap cycle, run the proof with `D12_PIN_GATE_ONLY=1`: it stops after P0's pin gate and exits non-zero when anything differs.
