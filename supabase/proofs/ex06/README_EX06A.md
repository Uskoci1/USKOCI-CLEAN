## The owner's definition (2026-10-01) and what follows from it
* "sutra" = the next local calendar day (23 or 25 hours on a DST change day); "ove nedelje" = the remaining part of the local Monday-Sunday week (the existing body clips the start to now); no invented hour, no narrower window.
* anchor = `published_at`; the anchor DAY is the local date in the zone of the window; zone = `task_timezone` when valid, else Europe/Belgrade (never the worker's; the worker's weekly rules are read on the worker's own zone).
* Plain consequences for the owner (not new decisions): **D1** live intent alone is not availability tomorrow or this week. **D2** both functions change (above). **D4** the day follows the publication instant: publishing at 23:59 or at 00:01 moves the matched day (as the lifecycle does); editing a task re-publishes it (published_at is cleared and set again), so editing after midnight moves "sutra"; the derived day is the whole local day, so "sutra popodne" also admits a worker free only in the morning (no hour is invented). **D5** a stored window stays exactly as today. **D6** a task without `published_at` keeps the refusal (no rolling anchor). **D7 (owner-visible)** the Discovery client (`src/data/marketplaceView.ts`, `workDays` and `happensIn`) reads "sutra" as the day after the VIEWING day, while the dispatch window and the lifecycle anchor on `published_at`: for a task published yesterday that nobody has accepted, a viewer sees "sutra" as today's tomorrow but the dispatch day was yesterday's tomorrow (= today). No change is made here; it is a consequence of anchoring on publication, the same anchor the lifecycle already uses.
* **D3 (owner-visible, chosen: the remaining part of the local week).** Two definitions meet: the lifecycle (`relative_schedule_end_v5`) keeps a WEEK task PUBLISHED for 7 days after publication; the dispatch window ends at the local Sunday midnight. After that it stays PUBLISHED for the rest of its 7 days with empty dispatch rounds (the manual application path reads only `responseAllowed`, never the schedule codes).
