## 2. What the proof does (`ex06_s03_proof.mjs` -> `lib/proof_main.mjs` `runProof`)
1. Harness self-tests (every gate class of the comparator reports a mismatch); pg_cron jobs `uskoci%` paused (a background tick must not run a wave before the harness does).
2. **Pin gate**: 11 of the 12 S01 function-body pins that stage 18 reaches (`lib/pins.mjs`; the 12th, `public.rpc_begin_push_send` fc76b344, is read after the extension by `ex06_s03_extension_report.mjs`). A difference is reported and downgrades the label; it never fails the run. Label: `MATCH/DISPATCH/EVENT FUNCTION BODIES == DEV (... ; helper functions, config rows and triggers not pinned)`. For information only (not pinned, not in the label): md5 of 10 dependency functions (`schedule_fit`, `worker_dispatch_time_admitted`, `worker_available_periods`, `availability_is_future`, `worker_calendar_conflict`, `lower_arr`, `haversine_km`, `effective_radius_km`, `identity_admitted`, `candidate_budget`), sha256 and wave sizes of the `marketplace_config` rows `dispatch_normal`, `dispatch_urgent`, `urgent_activation_policy`, and a catalog fingerprint (md5 of every function body and trigger of public and private; unchanged by the run, asserted).
3. **Certificate**: certified, live, erasure source and binding must be equal and ready at the start, unchanged at the end (harness error otherwise). The chain digest is compared with DEV's 58447d77 for information only (the chain stops before B24 part 2, voice B1 and pkg045b-p0).
4. **Canary** (first, mandatory, product path): fit + unfit (tool), a scheduled FIXED_WINDOW task (covering availability, vehicle), a worker in another city (radius), and the DRAFT control. Any disagreement is a `HARNESS_ERROR` (exit 1), never a finding.
5. **Per corpus case**: requester and workers through real Auth and the product's writers; foreign ACTIVE workers parked (restored at the end); the task through the **product path** (conversation -> synthetic proposals -> review with the city table's pins -> accept -> evaluate (synthetic ALLOW, no provider) -> publish); **read back** of the task and of every worker (`FIXTURE_NOT_APPLIED:<field>` otherwise); `private.match_detail(nid, pid)` per worker (the function the wave calls), shape-validated; the stored coordinates must give the matcher's `distanceToStartKm` (haversine within 0.02 km); ONE `private.dispatch_next_wave`, shape-validated (SENT, or STOPPED without `reason`; round 1 / policyWaveNo 1; the `dispatch_rounds` row must agree); deliveries and OPPORTUNITY_AVAILABLE events; comparison with the corpus; hidden kinds of the **stored** row; retire everything created (half-built workers and orphan tasks included).
6. Reports `ex06-s03-report.json` / `.md`, the `RESULT` line and workflow annotations.

## 3. Result, exit code, environment
`PASS | FINDINGS | SMOKE_ONLY | PARTIAL | HARNESS_BROKEN`. SMOKE never yields PASS; a capped run, a buildable case that did not run, or a DEGRADED case yields PARTIAL; a harness error is HARNESS_BROKEN (exit 1); findings exit 0 (2 with `EX06_STRICT_FINDINGS=1`). A green job is not evidence: read the `RESULT` line (`corpus=`, `cases ran/total`, `skipped`, asserted counts, `tasks: product/direct`, `unconsumed`, `findings`) or the `::warning` / `::error` annotations the proof prints.
`EX06_NEED_PATH` = `product` (workflow) | `direct` | `auto` (the last two are diagnostic: every such case is DEGRADED and listed with the facts the direct insert dropped). A product refusal of a READY case at review/accept/evaluate/publish is a FINDING `PRODUCT_PATH_REFUSED_READY_CASE`, never a fallback; a refusal at `open_conversation`/`provider_turns`, a timeout or an abort is a harness error. `EX06_REQUIRE_CORPUS=1` (workflow): no corpus file is an error. `EX06_CORPUS` (explicit file), `EX06_MAX_CASES` (positive integer), `EX06_STRICT_FINDINGS`, `EX06_ARTIFACT_DIR`, `EX06_STAGES_FILE`, `EX06_CATALOG_FILE`.

## 4. The S02 corpus (`lib/s02_adapter.mjs`, a TOTAL adapter)
`supabase/proofs/ai/corpus/ex06_contract_corpus_v1.json` (EX06_CONTRACT_CORPUS v1) is converted into the normalised shape of `lib/corpus.mjs`. Every key of the root, a case, `expected`, `expected.review`, `expected.kind`, a reference-worker row, its `profile` and its `expect` is MAPPED or IGNORED **with a reason** (the tables are exported and tested); a new key throws `S02_KEY_UNKNOWN`. What S03 cannot exercise is reported **UNCONSUMED**, never dropped. The numbers of the offline run against the real file (a tripwire in `s02_adapter.test.mjs`; update both when S02 changes):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| cases | 66: 37 mapped (all TASK, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`), **29 skipped with a reason** (16 WORKER family, 13 TASK that publish no task: T-016..021, T-033..035, T-039, T-040, T-045, T-049); ran + skipped == total |
| reference-worker rows | 150: 109 built, 41 not (39 in the skipped TASK cases, 2 calendar-busy workers) |
| **UNCONSUMED** | **80**: `busy` x2 (T-002, T-028: a calendar conflict needs an Agreement over the window), `applicationTimeBlockers` x7 (TEAM_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED is enforced when applying/selecting, not by the matcher), `eligibility:NOT_PUBLISHABLE` x18 and `NO_PUBLISHED_NEED` x21 (cases that publish nothing), `referenceTasks[].expect` x32 (WORKER family) |
| expectation leaves | 897 present, 545 consumed, 352 not consumed |
| key paths | 47 mapped, 60 ignored (each with a reason) |

Consumed per built worker: `responseAllowed`, `dispatchEligible`, `hardBlockers` (exact set), `dispatchBlockers` (exact set); `eligibility` is validated against them at load (a corpus that contradicts itself throws) and `unknownCapability` against the profile; `sourceReadingPrediction` (T-031) is carried as an annotation next to a finding. `kind.expected` (`unclassified` = nothing) is asserted against `private.work_kinds_v5(array_prepend(category, required_skills))` of the **stored** task (the input of the exclusion gate); the skills-only result (the service-match arm) is recorded next to it. The synthetic fact texts are built from the corpus' canonical items and `mustMention` words; the conversation `turns` are never used or copied. S02 has no delivery/event keys: they are checked as **derived invariants** (eligible => delivered; not eligible => not delivered; event iff delivery) and counted apart.
Findings the corpus itself predicts (author note A3: the corpus reads radius as city-name equality, the matcher uses geodesic distance when both positions exist; T-031 is a stem false positive) are expected S03 findings, not harness defects. Two things to read before judging a finding: a task of kind `TOMORROW_FLEXIBLE` / `WEEK_FLEXIBLE` that stores **no** `starts_at`/`ends_at` is refused for every worker by `worker_dispatch_time_admitted` (T-003 and T-006 would then show OUTSIDE_AVAILABILITY for the fit worker; the read-back records what the product stored), and calendar-busy workers are not built.

## 5. Fixture fidelity (`lib/fixtures.mjs`, `lib/geo.mjs`, `lib/timeutil.mjs`)
* **City table** (`geo.mjs`): Novi Sad, Petrovaradin, Futog, Beograd, Zrenjanin, Subotica, Sremska Mitrovica, Niš at two-decimal centres; used for the task pin (0.0025 degrees inside the cell, so round and floor agree) AND the worker position; an unknown city throws `EX06_CITY_UNKNOWN`. Niš - Novi Sad is about 272 km (inside the 300 km candidate search of the wave); Niš - Subotica (> 300 km) is outside it, which does not matter because that worker is outside the radius anyway.
* **Availability shapes** (`timeutil.availabilityFor`): AVAILABLE_NOW_AND_SCHEDULED / SCHEDULED_ONLY / AVAILABLE_NOW_ONLY / NONE_DECLARED -> the document the writer accepts; a covering weekly rule in the Europe/Belgrade weekday and wall clock (an AVAILABLE window across midnight or near a DST change).
* **Rebase**: every absolute `need.starts_at`/`ends_at` is shifted by `delta = CI now - corpus.clock.nowUtc` (offset text and duration kept; the delta is in the report); a window that is not at least 30 minutes ahead is `CASE_TIME_NOT_FUTURE`.
* **Workers**: `skills: []` ACTIVE workers are *activated, then cleared* (`rpc_complete_worker_profile` refuses an empty list; the owner UPDATE afterwards is a product state); `years_experience`, `exclusions`, `minimum_fee`, preferences by a labelled bypass (gap G04: no writer); `world: 'TEST'` through `rpc_admit_account_lineage_service`; nested profile keys are validated exactly.
* **Controls**: every case also builds a DRAFT copy of the fit worker (`control-restricted`), counted apart; a case without an eligible-worker anchor or without a negative that names its cause is a harness error in a contract corpus.
* **Counting**: corpus expectation fields (positive/negative, distinct gates), derived invariants, preconditions, controls, DEGRADED and UNREACHABLE_STATE are separate numbers.
